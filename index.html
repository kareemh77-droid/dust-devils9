Dust Devils

Two clans of goggle-wearing fennec foxes, one desert of very breakable sand. Last clan standing wins.

How to play: drag across the battlefield to aim. A longer drag means a harder shot. Then tap Fire. Wind pushes rockets and airstrikes, but not grenades. Grenades bounce and go off after 3 seconds. Anyone who falls in the quicksand is out.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger battlefield.
